# Product Context

## Why
Tier 1 support submits product questions through Jira. One product-support expert reviews each request, searches docs, responds, or escalates to product/engineering. This depends on one person's time and institutional knowledge.

## Problem
Make this workflow faster and more consistent without reducing trust or removing human judgment.

## Goals
- Answer from approved, current knowledge when possible, citing evidence.
- Ask for more information when the ticket is underspecified.
- Escalate to the right owner when human involvement is needed (e.g. active incidents, no reliable source).
- Keep the support expert in the loop: the copilot recommends, the human decides.

## Open product decisions
To be settled with the interviewer: who the output is for, what counts as a safe auto-response, handling of restricted/deprecated content.
